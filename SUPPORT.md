# Support

- **Bugs and feature requests**: open an issue at https://github.com/jstrullu/csharp-test-viewer/issues.
- Please include: the line starting with `⏱` and any error from the *C# Tests* output channel, your test framework and version, your .NET SDK version (`dotnet --version`), and your OS.
- For a wrong or unreadable failure display, the **Copy** button in the details panel gives everything needed (test name, message, stack trace).

This is a personal open-source project: issues are handled on a best-effort basis.
